# resumescreen-ai# ResumeScreen AI — AI Hiring Copilot
> An AI-powered browser application that helps recruiters move from **Job Description → Resume Screening → Recruiter Phone Screen → Interview Workflow → AI Hiring Debrief** in one place.

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://bilal127.github.io/resumescreen-ai/

The html code was generated & uploaded with the help of Claude AI.


## Why I built this
After years of recruiting across Engineering, Product and GTM roles, I realized that recruiters still spend significant time switching between multiple tools for screening, outreach, interview coordination and hiring decisions.

Most AI resume screening tools focus only on keyword matching. They rarely explain *why* a candidate is a fit or help recruiters throughout the rest of the hiring workflow.

I built **ResumeScreen AI** to better understand how AI can augment recruitment—not replace recruiter judgment. The objective was to combine recruiting fundamentals with product thinking and apply AI where it genuinely improves recruiter productivity, transparency and decision-making.

## What it does
ResumeScreen AI is a browser-based AI Hiring Copilot that supports recruiters throughout the hiring process.

### 📄 Job Description Intelligence
- Extracts recruiter-friendly hiring criteria from a JD
- Identifies:
  - Must-have skills
  - Good-to-have skills
  - Nice-to-have skills
  - Technical skills
  - Experience requirements
- Allows recruiters to review and edit AI suggestions before screening

### 📑 Explainable Resume Screening
- Screens multiple resumes against a JD
- Semantic skill matching (not just exact keywords)
- Transparent scoring
- Mandatory requirement validation
- Experience matching
- Recruiter-friendly score breakdown
- Resume keyword highlighting

### 🤖 AI Recruiter Copilot
Generates recruiter assistance including:

- Resume insights
- Match explanations
- Missing skills
- Recruiter phone screen questions
- JD-based interview questions

### 📞 Recruiter Workflow
- Personalized Email draft
- WhatsApp outreach draft
- Recruiter Phone Screen (RPS)
- Resume viewer
- Hiring pipeline tracking


### 🧑‍💼 Interview Management
Supports interview workflow including:
- Application
- Shortlist
- Round 1
- Round 2
- Debrief

Each stage maintains independent recruiter notes.
Interview rounds also support:
- Interviewer email
- Interview invitation
- Feedback reminders
- Interview feedback
- AI Hiring Debrief

### 🧠 AI Hiring Debrief
Consolidates interview feedback across hiring stages to help recruiters make informed hiring decisions.

Possible recommendations:
- Strong Hire
- Hire
- Hold
- Reject
- Strong Reject

## Design Principles
Rather than relying entirely on AI, the application combines:

- Recruiting fundamentals
- Explainable AI
- Product thinking
- Workflow automation
- Human decision-making

The goal is to augment recruiters—not replace them.
